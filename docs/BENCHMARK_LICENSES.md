# Benchmark licensing and provenance audit

Owner: Sub-agent A (Benchmark/Data Lead). Audited 2026-09-29.

Every licence below was read from the upstream artifact (GitHub licence API, HuggingFace dataset tags, or
the shipped `LICENSE` file) on the audit date, not recalled. Where a term is inferred rather than read, it
says so. **Re-verify before submission**: licences change, and a licence read once is a measurement with a
date on it.

## Summary table

| benchmark | code licence | data licence | gated | derived annotations redistributable on HF? | the catch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LongMemEval-**V2** | Apache-2.0 | Apache-2.0 | no | **Yes**, with attribution + NOTICE | screenshots are a separate large bundle |
| LongMemEval-**V1** | MIT | MIT | no | **Yes for the benchmark's own layer** | ~51% of sessions derive from ShareGPT/UltraChat, which carry their OWN upstream terms MIT does not override |
| BEAM | MIT | **CC BY-SA 4.0** | no | **Yes, but ShareAlike infects** — any derived dataset must itself be CC BY-SA 4.0 | the only licence here that constrains OUR release terms |
| SciCode | Apache-2.0 | Apache-2.0 | no | **Yes** for problems/tests | the 1.0 GB `test_data.h5` of numeric targets is on Google Drive with **no licence stated** |

**Consequence for a combined release.** BEAM's CC BY-SA 4.0 is incompatible with releasing one merged
Apache-2.0/MIT artifact. **Release our derived annotations as SEPARATE per-benchmark subsets**, each under
terms compatible with its source, rather than one pooled dataset under one licence.

## 1. LongMemEval-V2 — PRIMARY

- Data: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/xiaowu0162/longmemeval-v2> — HF tag `license:apache-2.0`,
  `gated: false`, `private: false`. VERIFIED via `https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/xiaowu0162/longmemeval-v2`.
- Code: <https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval-V2> — Apache-2.0 (GitHub licence API + `LICENSE` file read).
  Pinned commit `2cc8c540bdb87fe6761629b585e727e1c4704520` (2026-08-09).
- Paper: arXiv 2605.12493. Authors: Wu, Ji, Kawatkar, Kwan, Gu, Peng, Chang (UCLA).
- Data card states: "This dataset is released under the Apache License 2.0."

**Redistribution: PERMITTED.** Apache-2.0 allows redistribution and derivative works given attribution,
retention of the licence text, and a NOTICE of changes.

**What we redistribute and what we must not.**
- ✅ REDISTRIBUTE: our derived annotations — memory-unit boundaries (trajectory id + state index range),
  token counts, pairing manifests, leakage flags, DeltaU results. These are our measurements keyed to
  upstream ids.
- ✅ REDISTRIBUTE: the vendored `evaluation/qa_eval_metrics.py`, verbatim, with the Apache-2.0 header,
  upstream URL, commit and sha256 (done: `src/p1mem/adapters/vendor/`).
- ⚠️ DO **NOT** bundle the trajectory corpus or screenshots. Not a licence bar — Apache-2.0 would permit it —
  but `trajectories.jsonl` is 1.14 GiB and the screenshot bundles are far larger. Ship id references plus a
  download+checksum script. Users fetch from the canonical HF repo, which also keeps attribution intact.
- ⚠️ Question **screenshots** (31 PNGs) and trajectory screenshots are images of benchmark web
  environments. Covered by the same Apache-2.0 grant, but we have no need to redistribute them: our memory
  unit is text-only (`evidence_mode="axtree"`).

**Integrity.** The dataset ships `checksums.sha256`. `p1mem.adapters.lmev2.verify_checksums()` re-derives
and compares; `scripts/build_lmev2_units.py` refuses to run on a mismatch. VERIFIED OK on the audit date for
`questions.jsonl`, `trajectories.jsonl`, and both haystack files.

## 2. LongMemEval-V1 — SECONDARY (has evidence labels V2 lacks)

- Data: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/xiaowu0162/longmemeval> — HF tag `license:mit`.
- Code: <https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval> — MIT (GitHub licence API).

**🔴 The MIT grant does not reach all of the content.** LongMemEval-V1's haystacks are assembled from
multiple pools. Measured by session-id prefix over all 23,867 sessions in `longmemeval_s_cleaned.json`:

| pool | sessions | share | upstream terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sharegpt_*` | 6,179 | 25.9% | **ShareGPT** — scraped ChatGPT conversations. Redistribution status contested; OpenAI ToS issues; the widely used dumps carry no clean licence grant. |
| `ultrachat_*` | 6,008 | 25.2% | **UltraChat** (THUDM) — MIT on the repo, but generated with OpenAI models, so OpenAI ToS applies downstream. |
| 8-hex-prefix, e.g. `85a1be56_1` | **10,732** | **45.0%** | Appears LLM-generated by the benchmark authors (see note). Covered by the benchmark's MIT. |
| `answer_*` (evidence) | 948 | 4.0% | Authored by the benchmark. Covered by MIT. |

Note on the hex pool: **INFERRED, not read from upstream documentation.** Their modal turn count is 12
(10,662 of 10,732), matching the authors' `answer_*` evidence generator (modal 12), while the scraped pools
are broadly spread (sharegpt modal 2, ultrachat modal 8/14/12). Content register also matches. Treat as
author-generated pending confirmation; if it matters to a release decision, ask the authors.

**So: ~51.1% (12,187/23,867) of V1 sessions carry third-party upstream terms that MIT on the benchmark
repository does NOT override.** MIT covers the authors' own contribution — the questions, the evidence
sessions, the assembly — not the copyright status of scraped third-party dialogue.

**Redistribution rule for V1.**
- ✅ REDISTRIBUTE: our annotations keyed by `question_id` / `haystack_session_id` — leakage flags, unit
  definitions, DeltaU. These are measurements, not content.
- 🔴 DO **NOT** redistribute the `sharegpt_*` or `ultrachat_*` **session text** in any derived HF dataset.
  Reference them by id and let users obtain V1 from the canonical source.
- ⚠️ Do not redistribute verbatim session text from the hex pool either until its provenance is confirmed.
- ✅ Short quoted excerpts in the paper for illustration are ordinary fair-use academic quotation.

## 3. BEAM — SECONDARY / robustness

- Paper: arXiv 2510.27246, ICLR 2026 Poster (OpenReview note `y59hf5lrMn`).
- Code: <https://github.com/mohammadtavakoli78/BEAM> — **MIT** (GitHub licence API; `LICENSE` reads
  "MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025 Mohammad Tavakoli").
- Data: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/Mohammadta/BEAM> and `.../BEAM-10M` — HF tag
  **`license:cc-by-sa-4.0`**, `gated: false`.

**🔴 CC BY-SA 4.0 ShareAlike is the binding constraint in this project.** Attribution is easy; ShareAlike is
not. If we publish a derived dataset that incorporates BEAM content or adapted BEAM material, **that derived
dataset must itself be released under CC BY-SA 4.0**. It cannot be folded into an Apache-2.0 or MIT release.

**Redistribution rule for BEAM.**
- ✅ Publish BEAM-derived annotations as their **own HF dataset under CC BY-SA 4.0**, attributing Tavakoli
  et al. and linking the paper and source dataset.
- 🔴 Do **NOT** merge BEAM-derived rows into the same HF dataset as V1/V2/SciCode-derived rows. That either
  violates ShareAlike or silently relicenses the others.
- ℹ️ Mere *measurements about* BEAM (aggregate statistics, the diagonality table, DeltaU numbers) are facts,
  not adaptations, and are safe to report in the paper under any terms. The ShareAlike question bites when
  we ship conversation text or a transformed copy of the corpus.
- ⚠️ The BEAM data is synthetic and LLM-generated; the authors' own generation-model terms may apply
  upstream. Not investigated. Flagged, not resolved.

## 4. SciCode — TERTIARY / cross-domain transfer

- Paper: arXiv 2407.13168, NeurIPS 2024 Datasets & Benchmarks.
- Code: <https://github.com/scicode-bench/SciCode> — Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE` read; HEAD `e3158ea0`, 2025-10-06).
- Data: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/SciCode1/SciCode> — HF `license:apache-2.0`, `gated: false`.
  `problems_test.jsonl` (65) + `problems_dev.jsonl` (15).

**Redistribution: PERMITTED** for problems, subproblem prompts and test cases under Apache-2.0 with
attribution and NOTICE. No gating, no click-through, no contractual held-out split.

**🔴 The eval targets are the exception, and they are the part you actually need.**
The numeric expected outputs are NOT in the repo or the HF dataset. They ship as a ~1.0 GB `test_data.h5`
distributed through **Google Drive** (folder `1W5GZW6_bdiDAiipuFMqdUhvUaHIj6-pR`): unversioned, no checksum,
**no licence stamp**. An ungated HF mirror exists (`Srimadh/Scicode-test-data-h5`) which VERIFIED carries
**no licence tag at all**.

- 🔴 Do **NOT** redistribute `test_data.h5` or its contents. Its terms are unstated, and Apache-2.0 on the
  repository cannot be assumed to extend to a file distributed outside it.
- ✅ Mirror it privately for our own reproducibility and record a sha256 so our results are pinned to a
  specific copy — the upstream Drive file is unversioned and can change without notice.
- ✅ Redistribute our derived annotations (donor→recipient problem pairs, shared-function clusters, DeltaU
  results) keyed to upstream `problem_id` / `step_number`.
- ⚠️ Provenance note, not a licence bar: SciCode problems reproduce results from published papers. That is a
  citation obligation, not a redistribution restriction.

## What must be excluded from any HuggingFace release — checklist

1. ❌ `sharegpt_*` and `ultrachat_*` session text from LongMemEval-V1 (third-party upstream terms).
2. ⚠️ Hex-prefixed V1 session text, pending provenance confirmation.
3. ❌ SciCode `test_data.h5` contents (licence unstated).
4. ❌ BEAM content in any non-CC-BY-SA-4.0 release (ShareAlike).
5. ⚠️ Bulk LongMemEval-V2 trajectories/screenshots — permitted but ~1.14 GiB+; ship ids + a fetch script.
6. ✅ Always ship: upstream URL, pinned commit/revision, sha256, licence text, NOTICE of modifications.
