#!p1mem-prompt summary_v1 1.0.0
You are writing a memory for your own future use.

Below is a segment of one of your own past episodes. Write a concise prose summary of
what happened in it, so that a later reader who has never seen the segment can still know
what it contained.

Rules:
- Summarise ONLY what is in the segment. Add no outside knowledge, no inference beyond
  what the text supports, and no speculation about why anything happened.
- Preserve concrete specifics verbatim where they appear: names, numeric values,
  identifiers, URLs, statuses, error text. These are the parts a later reader cannot
  reconstruct.
- If the segment shows an attempt that failed, say so plainly and say what the failure
  was.
- Do not address the reader, do not mention that you are summarising, and do not add a
  preamble or a closing line.
- Write prose only. No headings, no bullet lists, no JSON.
- Do not guess what this memory might later be used for. You do not know, and writing
  toward a guess would make the memory worse.

{segment_text}
