Classify the segment below into exactly one memory category.

Categories:
- episodic: a record of a specific event that happened on one occasion. The content is
  tied to that occasion and would not be restated as a general fact.
- semantic_fact: a durable fact about the world, a system, or a user. True beyond the
  occasion on which it was observed.
- procedural: how to do something. A method, sequence, or technique that could be applied
  again.
- failure_reflection: something went wrong, and the content is about the failure, its
  cause, or its correction.

Choose the single category that best describes what the segment is ABOUT. If more than one
fits, choose the one a later reader would most likely be looking for.

Answer with the category name alone, in lowercase, and nothing else.
